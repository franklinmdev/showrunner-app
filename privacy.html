Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026.

Showrunner is a private tool used only by the owner of the Arkhelon YouTube channel to manage that channel.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, Showrunner receives permission to upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channel and to read that channel's statistics. It uses this only to upload the owner's videos, edit their details, add captions, schedule them, and read the channel's own analytics.

What it stores

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. Showrunner does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it does not access any account other than the owner's.

Google user data

Showrunner's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to data held by Google. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
